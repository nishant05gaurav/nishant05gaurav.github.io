02.

Who I Am

$ whoami

Hi, I'm Nishant Gaurav.

I’m a Computer Science Engineer specializing in Data Science who enjoys building software, solving problems, and understanding how technology works behind the code. I like moving between different parts of development — from writing algorithms and backend logic to working with APIs, databases, data and automation.

My foundation is built around Python, C++, Data Structures & Algorithms, OOP, DBMS, Operating Systems, Computer Networks and Linux. Alongside these fundamentals, I work with SQL, PostgreSQL, MySQL, Flask, REST APIs, Git and GitHub, with hands-on experience in Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, data analysis and AI-powered applications.

Most of my learning happens by building. Projects like GhostSpot, NOVA, recLog, Habit Flow Engine and Algorithm Analyzer have helped me explore different sides of software — including backend development, APIs, geospatial data, machine learning, automation, algorithms and application development.

log(Nishant) is another part of that process. I use it to document what I learn, break down concepts and share my problem-solving approach through technical writing. I also publish on platforms such as DEV and Medium. Writing helps me understand a topic better because explaining something clearly forces me to understand it properly.

Beyond individual projects, my experience as a Microsoft Learn Student Ambassador has given me opportunities to organize technical events, engage with developer communities and help other students learn and build. Right now, I’m focused on becoming a stronger software engineer while continuing to grow across backend development, data, AI, automation, problem solving and core computer science.
